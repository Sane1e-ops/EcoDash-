# AI Use Reflection
GitHub Copilot was used as an AI development assistant during the project.

Copilot assisted with implementing and checking several parts of the project, particularly the audio system and interface-related changes. I provided specific instructions about the features I wanted to implement, and the generated suggestions and changes were reviewed before being used.

For the audio implementation, Copilot helped connect the supplied audio files to different gameplay events. This included a looping background track during gameplay, a startup sound, delivery audio and an achievement sound after completing the five-delivery objective. Copilot also helped ensure that audio responded correctly to pause, game-over and the sound toggle.

Copilot also assisted with checking asset paths, HTML references, mission progression, the objective counter and the existing five-delivery victory condition.

The AI was used as an assistant rather than a replacement for development and testing. Changes were reviewed and tested to ensure that the final implementation worked correctly with the existing game.
