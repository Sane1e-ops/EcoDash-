# African Context Report

EcoDash uses an African-inspired fictional environment to provide context for the game's logistics and infrastructure theme.

The game explores concepts such as:

Infrastructure availability
Distance between locations
Energy and charging access
Weather conditions
Transportation and delivery challenges
The importance of reliable logistics

The setting is intentionally fictional and should not be interpreted as a representation of every African country, community or infrastructure system. The African context is used to create a relevant setting for exploring the broader relationship between technology, infrastructure, energy and logistics.

The project therefore focuses on the general challenges and possibilities of technology-enabled delivery systems rather than attempting to accurately represent a specific African community.
